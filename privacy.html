Privacy Policy, The Family Stack Uploader

Effective: October 19, 2026

  1. Who we are. The Family Stack Uploader ("the App") is a private tool run by the owner of The Family Stack YouTube channel for that channel only.
  2. What the App accesses. With the channel owner's permission through Google OAuth, the App uses the YouTube Data API to upload and manage videos, captions, thumbnails and video details on the owner's channel, and to read that channel's video and caption information to check uploads.
  3. How data is used. Data is used only to publish and maintain content on the owner's own channel. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone else.
  4. Storage. OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's private server, with access limited to the owner's own automation. No other personal data is collected.
  5. Sharing. None. We do not sell, rent, trade or disclose data to third parties.
  6. Google and YouTube. The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  7. Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the App is retired.
  8. Children. The App is not for children and collects no data from viewers.
  9. Changes. Updates are posted on this page with a new effective date.
  10. Contact: Keeninklings@gmail.com
